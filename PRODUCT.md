# Impeccable Design Context

## Design Context

### Users
FDCP (Film Development Council of the Philippines) staff — government employees
booking rooms/vehicles and managing a shared events calendar during office
hours. Admins review registrations and approvals. Internal tool, desktop-first,
used daily.

### Brand Personality
Calm, credible, quietly cinematic. A government agency for FILM — trustworthy
first, with a soft creative undercurrent. Not flashy, not corporate-sterile.

### Aesthetic Direction
Soft-pastel product language chosen from a reference (Notion/linear-calendar
style): light gray canvas, floating white rounded panels, near-black primary
pills, pastel event colors (sky, lavender, pink, mint, cream) with hue-matched
dark ink, and a soft blue brand wash on the sidebar (radial glow, hand-tuned in
app.css). Poppins across the UI. Both light and dark themes supported; light is
the default working context.

Anti-references: AI-purple gradients, neon-on-dark, glassmorphism-everywhere,
vivid yellow as chrome (retired earlier in favor of the pastel system).

### Design Principles
1. Trust before delight — nothing that undermines a government tool's credibility.
2. Color belongs to content (events, calendars), not chrome; chrome stays neutral.
3. One accent moment per surface; black pills carry the primary action.
4. Motion is calm, physical, and always honors prefers-reduced-motion.
5. The film heritage may whisper (light, projection, frames) but never shout.
